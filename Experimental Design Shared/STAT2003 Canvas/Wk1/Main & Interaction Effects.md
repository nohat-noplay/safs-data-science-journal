
**Main Effects:**
Individual influence of each factor on the response $$Y = \mu + A + B + \epsilon$$
Changing the level of factor $A$ affects the response (regardless of $B$) 

**Interaction Effects** ($2^2$ Factorial design):
The influence of one factor depends on the level of another factor $$Y = \mu + A + B + AB + \epsilon$$
If $AB \neq 0$, the effect of A on the response ($Y$) changes depending on the level of $B$
