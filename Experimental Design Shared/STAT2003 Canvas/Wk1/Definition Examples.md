
**Factors**: _Type of Fertilizer, Watering Frequency_

**Levels**: 
1. _Type of Fertilizer (3 levels): "Organic", "Chemical", "No Fertilizer"
2. _Watering Frequency: 2 levels (Daily, Every Other Day)_

**Treatments**: 
_Since we have 3 fertilizer types × 2 watering frequencies = **6 different treatments**,
	1 treatment would be: 1. "Organic" + "Daily"

**Experimental Unit**: _the individual tomato plant_

**Response**: _total weight in grams of tomatoes produced by plant after 8 weeks_

**Main Effects**: 
$$Y = \mu + \text{Fertiliser} + \text{Watering Freq.} + \epsilon$$
_eg. weight of tomatoes affected by fertilizer regardless of watering_

**Interaction Effects**: 
$$\begin{aligned}
Y = \mu &+ \text{Fertiliser} + \text{Watering Freq.} \\
        &+ (\text{Fertiliser} \cdot \text{Watering Freq.}) + \epsilon
\end{aligned}$$
_organic fertilizer only increases yield when watering is daily, but has little effect when watering is every other day_ 

**Randomisation**:
_Each tomato plant is selected to receive a treatment at random - OD, CE, ND, OE, NE, etc._

**Replication**: 
_ There are 30 plants and 6 treatments, 5 plants will receive the same treatment_

**Blocking**:
_Amount of sunlight in planting areas is an uncontrolled variability - divide planting areas into blocks depending on sunlight and apply all treatments in each block_
