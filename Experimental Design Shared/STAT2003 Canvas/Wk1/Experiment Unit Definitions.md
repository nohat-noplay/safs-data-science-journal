
**Factors**
The variables in an experiment that are controlled or varied to observe their effect on the response.

**Levels**
The specific settings or values that a factor can take in the experiment.

**Treatments**
The combinations of factor levels applied to the experimental units. Number of levels for 1 factor $\times$ Number of levels for 2nd factor $\times$ etc.

**Experimental Unit**
The smallest division of the experimental material to which a treatment is independently applied.

**Response**
The outcome or result measured in the experiment to assess the effect of the treatments.
