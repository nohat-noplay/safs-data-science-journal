** Full Factorial experiment** studies **two or more factors simultaneously**, each at **two or more levels**, and observes their **main and interaction effects** on a response.
- Efficient: Evaluates multiple factors in a single experiment
- Captures interactions between factors
- Common format: $2^k$ design (k factors, each at 2 levels) 
- _eg. $2^2$ factorial = 2 factors $\times$ 2 levels = 4 treatments_
