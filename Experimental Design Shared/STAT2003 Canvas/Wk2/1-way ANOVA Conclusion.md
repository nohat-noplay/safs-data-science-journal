> ❌ **One-way ANOVA does not account for**:
- Effects of **multiple factors** (_e.g., both fertilizer _and_ watering frequency),
- **Interactions** between factors (_e.g., whether fertilizer effect changes depending on how often you water_).
To test those, you'd need:
> **Two-way ANOVA** or a **factorial design**
